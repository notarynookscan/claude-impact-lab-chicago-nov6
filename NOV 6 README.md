This is the home base for today's build. Everything you need is here: the problem, the rules, how you will be judged, and how to submit.

## Our partner

Future House is a South Holland nonprofit that provides transitional housing and social services. Everything built today is open-sourced on GitHub and donated to Future House, so your work keeps serving residents long after tonight.

## The challenge: close the gap to services

Residents who qualify for help with housing, food, jobs and legal questions often never reach it. Build a working tool that closes that gap for one real person or organization.

Pick one area to start:

- **Housing:** finding and keeping a safe place to live
- **Food:** reaching pantries, benefits and meals
- **Jobs:** training, openings and getting hired
- **Legal help:** plain answers to legal questions

## Ground rules

1. **Teams of 4 to 5.** Teams were formed in advance. Stay with your table unless we rebalance for no-shows.
2. **No pre-work.** Everyone starts from zero at the same time. Fresh code only.
3. **Name who it is for.** A real person or organization your tool serves. Judges will ask.
4. **Open source everything.** Build in a public GitHub repository and link it in your submission.

No-code builds count. Stuck? Mentors are in the room all day. Raise a hand or post in the Discord.

3. Scan the credits QR code and submit your Org ID. One claim per person.

5. In the Console, create an API key for today's build. Never commit your key to GitHub.

## How to submit

One submission per team, by **4:30 PM**.

1. Push your code to your team's own **public** GitHub repository. Add a short README that says what it does and who it is for.
2. Optional, but strongly encouraged: record a **two-minute demo** (the problem, the build, who it helps) and add the link.
3. Submit through the form: [Impact Lab Nov 6 Prototype Submission](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1vQ6zSd5EQUVkZMuz3_mqII_vh-e8no-v9dCNOih0j20/viewform), or scan the submission QR code on the slides.

The form asks for your team name, every member's full name and college email, your public repo link, your demo link, and two or three sentences on what you built and who it is for.

## Judging

| Weight | Criterion | What judges look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30% | Real problem and recipient | A specific Chicago-area problem and a named person or organization it is for |
| 30% | Usefulness and impact | Could that recipient actually use it on Monday? |
| 20% | What you built | A real, working tool, guide or analysis, with Claude used well |
| 20% | Demo and story | In about two minutes: the problem, the build and who it helps |

Ties go to real problem and recipient first, then usefulness and impact.

**Judges:** Janella Curtis, Founding Claude Community Ambassador, Chicago; Todd Walton, Claude Community Ambassador, Chicago; and special guest judge State Senator Napoleon Harris III, Illinois 15th District.
